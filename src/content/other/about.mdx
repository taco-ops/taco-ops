I build and tend cloud infrastructure, chiefly on Kubernetes, for places where failed deployments have consequences less poetic than they are expensive. My work has moved through Marvel post-production systems at Disney, observability for autonomous robots at Fox Robotics, and now cloud-native platforms at Silicon Labs, where clusters, GPUs, pipelines, and human expectations compete for the same finite patience.

I am also an astrophotographer. On clear nights I collect faint light through a telescope and spend the rest of the process proving which parts of it deserve belief. Bad frames, clouds, satellites, thermal noise, and optimistic assumptions all receive the same treatment: inspection first, sentiment afterward.

Infrastructure rewards the same disposition. The useful work is rarely glamorous. It is calibration, failure handling, observability, and the refusal to trust an abstraction merely because it arrived with a confident diagram.
